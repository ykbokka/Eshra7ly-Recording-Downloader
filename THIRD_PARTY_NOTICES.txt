THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS

Eshra7ly Downloader bundles third-party software for standalone operation.

  Distributed from the Windows essentials build published by Gyan Doshi:
  https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
  The upstream FFmpeg license notice for the exact packaged build is embedded
  with the executable resources as licenses/FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt.
  FFmpeg licensing depends on the configuration of the upstream build. Review
  the bundled notice and upstream license information before redistribution.

  The Playwright Python package and bundled browser components are subject to
  their upstream license and trademark notices.

Chromium

  Chromium includes third-party components with their own notices. Upstream
  Chromium license and third-party notice information is maintained by the
  Chromium project.
